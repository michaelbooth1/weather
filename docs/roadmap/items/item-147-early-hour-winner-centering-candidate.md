# 147. Early-Hour Winner-Centering Candidate [PARTIAL 2026-06-22 - DISPOSITION REFRESHED, SHADOW-ONLY BASELINE]

Goal: build and evaluate an early-hour candidate that improves winner-band
centering and rank, not just generic probability sharpness.

Source: `data/backtest/hourly_model_performance_report.md` generated by
`src/weather/reporting/hourly_model_performance.py` schema
`hourly_model_performance_v0.3`. The deep diagnostics show that 00:00-08:00
has model Brier `0.0670` versus market Brier `0.0511`; the model assigns the
eventual winner `29.4%` versus the market's `43.5%`, spreads over `4.54`
effective bands versus the market's `3.46`, and ranks the winner `+0.89` bands
worse. In the worst hours, 03:00-05:00, the market gives the eventual winner
about `10.0` percentage points more probability and ranks it `+0.56` bands
better on average.

Why this matters: the partition-power remediation probe barely changes
early-hour Brier, so the failure is not solved by broad sharpening or
softening. The model needs to move probability toward the right forecast-relative
bands before the observed-temperature path becomes informative.

## Design

1. Add a candidate objective or postprocessor that explicitly scores
   winner-rank, winner-probability gap, adjacent-winner mass, and effective-band
   spread in 00:00-08:00 snapshots.
2. Use forecast-relative geometry as the anchor: `forecast_high`, bucket
   distance from forecast, forecast-gap direction, source count, forecast
   disagreement, ensemble spread, and run-to-run changes.
3. Penalize probability that is spread across many bands when source agreement
   is strong, but avoid naive sharpening when forecast disagreement is high.
4. Evaluate separately from market-aware overlays so this remains a no-market
   weather-model improvement path.
5. Feed the candidate into item 145's hourly gate and coordinate with items
   134, 135, and 136 for forecast-profile and source-state inputs.

- [x] Add winner-rank, winner-probability, adjacent-mass, and effective-band
  metrics to the hourly audit output.
- [x] Build a replay-only forecast-relative early-hour centering probe.
- [x] Promote the forecast-centering probe into a candidate or postprocessor.
- [x] Compare against current, item 134 forecast-profile, item 135 regime-weighted,
  and market benchmark on daily-first market-day validation.
- [ ] Require early-hour lift without midday/late regression beyond promotion
  tolerance.
- [x] Add a casebook of early snapshots where the market ranked the winner well
  and the model spread probability across adjacent or wrong-side bands.

Acceptance: a no-market candidate improves 00:00-08:00 winner probability,
winner rank, adjacent-winner mass, and Brier versus current on daily-first
settled evidence, while preserving the no-market/market-aware separation used
by the hourly audit.
