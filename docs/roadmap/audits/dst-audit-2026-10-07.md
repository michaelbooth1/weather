## 1. Verdict

- **Settlement and WU parsing are DST-safe.** WU history keys rows by `valid_time_gmt` epoch converted to the market
  zone (`sources/wu_history.py:997-1002`, `model/model_sources.py:1051-1054`), and settlement ledgers key on that local
  date. No hard-coded UTC offset exists in code. Every hour gate (07:00 max-since rule, 13:00 partial and 17:00 lock-in,
  cutoff hours 7-20) is local wall time at an hour that occurs exactly once on both DST days.
- **Two CRITICAL defects can cost capture or scheduled runs from 2026-11-01:**
  - **DST-C1:** every daily Scheduler trigger stores a fixed -04:00 offset in its StartBoundary (a fixed UTC
    instant); the stored form was observed on the capture host on 2026-10-07. From 11-01 each such task probably fires
    one hour earlier on the local clock (inferred; not testable before 11-01), so the cold-snapshot nightly and the
    Stage-B evidence refresh are refused every night.
  - **DST-C2:** the METAR look-back loses the first local hour on the 25-hour day. PR #196 fixes it.
- **Four HIGH defects corrupt grading or abort ops in the transition hour:**
  - DST-H1: the 11-01 graded window is shifted one hour early.
  - DST-H2: early-hour coverage falsely BLOCKs every market on 03-14.
  - DST-H3: a false merge rollback is possible in the quiet window.
  - DST-H4: the bounded suite aborts on a false "capture unhealthy" reading.
- **The common root cause is wall-clock arithmetic on aware values.** In Python, two aware datetimes that share one
  `tzinfo` object (every `ZoneInfo` is cached, so `spec.tz` is always the same object) subtract and compare on the wall
  clock. In PowerShell, `[datetime]"...-04:00"` converts to local wall time, drops the offset, and subtraction is
  wall-clock.

Severity counts: **CRITICAL 2, HIGH 4, MEDIUM 10, LOW 16.**
