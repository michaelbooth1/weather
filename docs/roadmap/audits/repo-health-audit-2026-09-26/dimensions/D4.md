## Proposed ratchets

1. **Tracked-file size budget** (a test over `git ls-files -s` + `cat-file --batch-check`): fail on any new blob over 1 MiB outside an explicit
   allowlist (today: `location_market_events.json`, `identity-binding-…-76a-manifest.json`, `nbm_target_trace/…/artifact-selectors.json`), and
   require a mission id in the filename for new `docs/roadmap/*.{csv,json,jsonl}` over 250 KB. This would have caught D4-5.
2. **Artifact registry coverage**: every `git ls-files artifacts/**` (excluding `manifests/` and `AGENTS.md`) appears in `model_artifact_registry.json`
   with a matching sha256 or LFS oid. The preflight's "manifest identity drift" may already do this; confirm before adding.
3. **Generated-config commit guard**: after D4-1, a test that no tracked `config/*.json` is written by a scheduled generator (the
   `config_inventory.py` classification already names "Generated snapshot"; assert that class lives outside `config/`).
