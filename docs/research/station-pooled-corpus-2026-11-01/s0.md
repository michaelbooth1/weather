## 3. Labels by the venue rule (computable from METAR alone)

The venue-rule label (AGENT_CONTEXT § Settlement and units, "Training and evaluation labels … from 2026-10-23"; contract
[venue-rule labels](../../operations/venue-rule-labels.md) § Rule) is the highest **routine** METAR on the local date,
SPECI excluded, remark T-group tenths with a body fallback, half-up to whole °F. `weather.backtesting.venue_rule_labels`
(`reports_from_csv`, `select_reports`, `venue_daily_labels`) computes it from an IEM `report_type=3` file with raw text,
and `weather.calibration.new_city_corpus` already reuses it for non-built-in stations with the 180-minute completeness
rule (NEW_CITY_MODEL_LANE § Label and print-cutoff rules). The post-10-23 SPECI exclusion is native: routine and special
are separate requests, and the sample's routine files hold no `SPECI`-prefixed line. Requirements for pooled stations:

- a duck-typed spec (zone, unit F, station) instead of a registry row (do **not** add them to
  `config/candidate_markets.json`, which would make them candidate markets);
- exclude stations with T-group share < 0.95 (labels would be whole-°C, coarser than the 11 markets' labels);
- US ASOS routine reports are hourly at the reset minute, so no `iem_special_schedule_minute` is needed (KDEN-like).

**Label mismatch to disclose:** the 11 markets' 2021-25 training and scoring labels stay the WU history the venue paid
(venue-rule labels § Label reader); pooled stations get routine-METAR labels. On 08-11..09-29 the two rules disagree on
13 of 517 market-days and move the band on 8 (same doc, "Not changed").

## 4. Corpus builder plan (for S1, after master's go)

1. **Station list (outcome-free, frozen before any fetch):** the 1,365 ex-Gulf candidates (`s0_candidate_ids.txt`),
   then, after the IEM fetch and before any label is read, drop stations with routine T-group share < 0.95, label-complete
   share < 0.90 of Aug-Dec 2021-25 local days, or no data. Choose N by the rule master picks (my proposal: all survivors,
   with a pre-declared random 300-station subset as the dose arm; never chosen by score).
2. **IEM METAR** (free, official, owner-approved source family): per station and year one `report_type=3` and one
   `report_type=4` request, UTC Aug 1..Jan 1 12Z, `tmpf` + `metar`, serial, 5 s apart, resumable, receipts with sha256
   (the `venue_rule_labels` `<icao>/{routine,special}/` layout). 300 stations: 3,000 requests, ≈ 0.85 GB raw (≈ 0.2 GB
   gzip), ≈ 7 h. 1,000 stations: 10,000 requests, ≈ 2.9 GB raw (≈ 0.6 GB gzip), ≈ 23 h. Runs directly (network-bound),
   not in the heavy wrapper, as the L9 and M1 fetches did.
3. **NBM NBP 07Z:** at hundreds of stations L9's per-station HTTP Range fetcher reads about as many bytes as the whole
   bulletin with ~3 requests per station, so S1 uses a **new** streaming extractor (a v2 file, never an edit of
   `tools/research/ols_floor_ladder_nbm_fetch.py`): download each 26.7-34.8 MB bulletin on L9's 675 dates, record S3
   LastModified, size, ETag and sha256, write the selected stations' blocks (≈ 3.0-3.8 KB each) to gzip JSONL, delete the
   bulletin. ≈ 21.6 GB transferred, ≈ 3.3 GB raw blocks for all 1,365 candidates (≈ 0.5 GB gzip), peak disk one
   bulletin; ≈ 2-6 h (transfer rate not measured). The PIT rule is L9's (availability = LastModified).
4. **Rows:** a new research module (proposed `weather.calibration.station_pool_corpus`, outside serving/capture/release,
   with the same production-import refusal as `pooled_residual_model`) builds P3d_N-format rows for each (station,
   date in P3d_N's centre windows, hour): `y` venue-rule label, `F` METAR running max since local midnight (all reports,
   as `observed_running_max.running_max_since_midnight(..., metar_only=True)` defines it), `x_current_temp` latest
   METAR at or before h:00, `fh` the NBP 07Z v2 mean at the cutoff (L9's `nbm_at_cutoff` rule), lat/lon/elev from IEM,
   doy terms, `market` = station id, `season`/`centre`/`year`. B-bound modules (`pooled_residual_model`,
   `mg2_p3d_n`, `observed_running_max`) are imported read-only, never edited. The parser needs a zone per station; the
   module passes IEM's zone in-process (the S0 check did the same), it does not edit `NBM_NBP_STATION_TIMEZONES`.
5. **Where:** raw fetch under `C:\t\l48\corpus\raw\` (or `data\research\station_pool\`, the L6 precedent, if master
   prefers durable runtime state); the built corpus published immutable and content-addressed
   (`corpora/<sha256>/rows.jsonl.gz`, `manifest.json`) like `new_city_corpus`. Free space on C: was 32.9 GB at S0, so
   bulletins are never retained.
6. **Fit cost (estimate):** P3d_N fits one mean HGB, four inner-LOYO HGBs and one scale HGB per hour and fold; at 300
   stations each sees ≈ 160-200k rows (vs ≈ 6k), ≈ 180 fits for 07-12 LOYO, plus the kernel distribution whose `z` set
   grows ~27x. Order 1-2 h at 300 stations, 3-6 h at 1,000, under `workstation_heavy.ps1 -Queue`.

## 5. Mechanism (rule 14) and my assessment

The brief's mechanism: individual traders cannot estimate per-station conditional NBM error distributions from a few
seasons; a ~100x corpus can. Where this would act: P3d_N's bias, OLS-on-floor-gap and HGB residual (centre), its HGB
scale model and its kernel of standardised errors (shape). Pooling 1 → 11 markets was the one structural lever that
worked (U0 vs P3 +0.174 [+0.141, +0.208], EF §10aa), so the lever is the next step on a measured axis, not a new source.

Assessment (mine; inference, not measurement): this is a **parity** lever with a weak edge story.

- NBM's NBP percentiles and TXNSD are already a station-level calibrated distribution that traders can read for
  free (UNVERIFIED-EXTERNAL: NBM's quantile post-processing is itself trained on multi-station analyses), so the
  "100x data" advantage is partly inside the input the market already sees; NBM-published spread is closed at dev
  (R-S, register X37).
- Canon's inferred market advantage is observation speed plus *day-specific* confidence (FINDINGS_DIGEST, research
  review takeaway 2); a pooled corpus sharpens *climatological* conditional error, and the R-H atlas found the 07-12 loss
  regime-flat (EF §10ag).
- Best case it narrows MG-1/P3d_N's centre and shape gap (07-12 R 1.19 for P3d_N, EF §10ad). It does not by itself
  supply information the market lacks.

## 6. Counter-evidence

1. **L1 full-year corpus adds nothing:** P3F − P3 −0.004 [−0.014, +0.006] (EF §10aa; corpus `621bcd8e`, 245,625 rows).
   The axis differs (more months at the same 11 stations vs more stations in the same months), but it shows the pooled
   model is not row-starved at 11 markets. X1 (EF §10z): "More rows do not rescue" R0.
2. **Version shift, not sample size, may drive the scale failures:** NBP is V4.0/V4.1/V4.2/V4.3 across 2021-25 and V5.0
   at serve; R-I's walk-forward temperature 1.40-1.88 vs LOYO 1.04-1.29 did not transfer (EF §10af), X64 could not
   separate no-information from no-transfer to V5.0, and NBS needs a per-version check (register X35, X64;
   FINDINGS_DIGEST R-L2). A 2021-25 corpus at 100x stations deepens the V4.x fit; it does not address V5.0.
3. **The dev gate kills small LOYO gains:** ten rungs passed LOYO and died at dev; dev MDE80 is ≈ 0.005-0.006 at 07-12
   (EF §10af/§10ak; LEVER_INVENTORY). A LOYO pass here is likely to meet the same wall.
4. **Other stations' information is already in NBM:** upstream ASOS ring observations KILL as features (X11, EF §10ab,
   "NBM already carries it"). Different use (features, not training rows), same caution.
5. **Against the market every NBM-anchored arm has w ≈ 0** (EF §10ad). A better weather-only model is not edge.

## 7. Gate design (DRAFT for master; not a frozen rule)

- **Arms (one candidate, no best-of):** `P3d_N_11m` = P3d_N's LOYO pipeline on the METAR-path rows of the 11 markets
  (the base); `P3d_N_pool` = the identical code with the pooled stations' rows added to every training fold. Only the
  training rows differ.
- **Blocked LOYO 2021-25 by year across all stations:** the held-out year is removed from every station, so no
  same-date neighbour row trains a fold.
- **Scored on the 11 markets only**, their canonical labels (WU 2021-25, venue-paid). Pooled stations never enter a
  score.
- **Primary:** 07-12 Brier, `P3d_N_pool − P3d_N_11m`, date-clustered bootstrap (2,000 draws) plus the block-3 interval
  (method rule 7). **Kill** if the point estimate is not below −0.0005 or the wider upper bound is ≥ 0. Also kill if
  any market is CI-worse or more than one year has the opposite sign. Secondary (reported, not deciding): 13-16 Brier,
  07-12 log loss, per-market and per-year tables.
- **Controls (before the candidate statistic):** (C-a) `P3d_N_11m` on the METAR-path rows vs the existing WU-path P3d_N
  LOYO rows: report the delta (feature-path parity); (C-b) w = 0 identity: with zero pooled rows `P3d_N_pool` equals
  `P3d_N_11m` byte for byte; (C-c) the venue-rule label vs WU on the 11 markets' 2021-25 days: agreement rate reported;
  (C-d) per-version outcome-free check: pooled-station NBP block parse rate and v2 mean/stddev distribution by version.
- **Reported, not deciding:** a 300-station random-subset dose arm and a "pool the scale model and kernel only" arm.
- **Next tiers only if gate 1 passes:** dev 07-12 dR vs P3d_N_fix (gate 2), then walk-forward w vs placebo and
  floor-only (method rule 4). Training years stay 2021-25; using V5.0-era 2026 rows from pooled stations is **not**
  proposed (they share dates and weather with the 11 markets' dev window; see §8 item 3).
- **Power:** to be computed before the rule freezes from P3d_N's LOYO paired SD on the existing rows (outcome-free with
  respect to the candidate); none computed in S0.
