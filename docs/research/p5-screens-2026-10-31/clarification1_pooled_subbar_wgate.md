  recorded commit and its recorded run env, on dev dates only.
- **A pinned emit is valid only after C4.** A pinned emit file and its sha256 count only once that member has
  passed C4.

  row (rule section 2, last column), to 1e-9 or to its recorded rounding.

## 2. C5 SYN+ uses a calibrated plant, not a fixed multiple

The plant replaces section (a)'s fixed `SYN_SHRINK` 0.5. Its form is unchanged:
- Gaussian bands centred at μ_m + sign · s · (y_mid − μ_m) + ε, width σ_m;
- ε ~ N(0, sd(y_mid − μ_m)), seeded, with the same ε for both signs;
- it is pooled at the block's member count.

Only the scale s is calibrated.

### Calibration

The calibration uses its own toy draw, separate from the verification draws. It is written before it runs.

- **Toy.** The module test's synthetic generator:
  - 34 dates, 10 markets, hours 0-16, 5 bands;
  - market vector p_m ~ Dirichlet(2·1);
  - outcome probabilities q ~ Dirichlet(4·p_m + 1e-3), i.e. **market concentration 4**;
  - fit = all 34 dates, score = dates 5-34;
  - 1,000 bootstrap draws.
- **Draw seed:** 20261031.
- **Grid:** s from 0.50 to 2.00 in steps of 0.05, in increasing order.
- **Frozen s** = the smallest s on the grid whose toy population SYN+ Δ (the point estimate) is ≥ 0.25 in
  every block (POOL-0712 at 5 members, F2-1316 at 1, POOL-0006 at 2). The target 0.25 is above Δ_edge 0.2236.
- If no grid value reaches 0.25, nothing is frozen, C5 cannot be calibrated, and the result goes to master.

### C5 pass rule (per block)

C5 passes iff all three hold:
1. the SYN+ lower 95% bound > 0;
2. the SYN+ wider upper 95% bound ≥ 0.2236;
3. the SYN− Δ upper 95% bound < 0.

"Lower" means the smaller of the date-clustered and block-3 lower bounds; "upper" and "wider upper" mean the
larger of the two upper bounds.

### Verification before freezing

The frozen s is verified at market concentration 4 on toy draw seeds 11 (the module test's draw), 12 and 13,
none of which is the calibration seed. C5 must pass in every block on all three draws. If any fails, s is not
frozen and the result goes to master.

The toy script's sha256, its elapsed time and its peak memory are recorded in the freeze addendum below. A toy
over 1 minute or 1 GB goes through `workstation_heavy.ps1 -Queue`.
