## Mechanics and limits established before scanning

[Official negRisk documentation](https://docs.polymarket.com/concepts/negative-risk) describes
NO-to-complementary-YES conversion. The
[adapter implementation](https://github.com/Polymarket/neg-risk-ctf-adapter/blob/main/src/NegRiskAdapter.sol)
supports converting m NO positions into m-1 units of collateral plus complementary YES positions,
and can deduct a conversion fee. The scanner does not assume a reverse conversion.

[Official fees](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/fees) agree with EF section 10o:
weather takers pay `0.05 * shares * p * (1-p)` and makers pay zero.
[Order constraints](https://docs.polymarket.com/trading/place-orders) require the captured
tick size and minimum order size. Dated downloaded source bytes and hashes are retained in
`out\sources\` and `out\official_sources_receipt.json`; these current docs are not proof of
each historical market's adapter fee or full configuration.

The supplied event folders contain `order_books_summary.csv`, token CSV excerpts and, for
some dates, public execution tapes. The scan only opens the summaries. Their 10/100-share
VWAPs were generated by walking depth (`vwap_for_size` in
`src/weather/market/market_microstructure_capture.py`). They do not retain individual fill
prices: nonlinear taker fees need bounds unless all shares fit at best. Exact 1-share costs
are available only when top depth suffices. A one-share request below the recorded minimum
cannot count as executable.

The capture function assigns `captured_at_utc` before fetching the books. A shared timestamp
is not a receipt-time simultaneity guarantee. The source `book_timestamp` is not measured
observation-to-order latency. Source-state spans of 1/5/30/60 seconds and consecutive-sample
reobservations will be reported, without treating them as continuous opportunity duration
or latency survival. Group identity, adapter fee, raw fill prices and HTTP receipt/latency
receipts remain necessary to confirm an executable case.
