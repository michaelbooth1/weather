How the model works:
- Remaining rise r = (METAR day max) - (PIT running max), integer F, clipped to 0..30. P(r | cell) is an empirical histogram
  with recursive shrinkage toward the parent cell, K = 20 pseudo-rows.
- History: IEM METAR, local dates 2024-05-01..2026-07-31, months within +-1 of the target month, complete days only (at least
  20 routine rows). That is 184-215 days per station-month, sampled on a 15-min grid. The history grid and the snapshots use
  the same feature function, so train/serve parity holds.
- Bins:
  - deficit: {0, 1, 2, 3-4, 5+}
  - slope1h (vs the obs valid at least 50 min earlier, age at most 90 min): {<=-2, -1, 0, +1, >=+2, na}
  - slope3h (at least 170 min earlier, age at most 210 min): {<=-4, -3..-1, 0..2, 3..5, >=6, na}
  - sky (maximum cover in the latest obs): {CLR/FEW, SCT, BKN/OVC/VV, na}
  - wind: {<6, 6-12, >12 kt, na}
- Band probability = P(M + r falls in the band), with eq = [low, high], lte <= high, gte >= low.
- The harness applies the rule-4 floor and the served fallback.

## Point-in-time statement

- Every input is a METAR or SPECI row from the A-IEM manifest (`C:\swarm\data\iem\metar\MANIFEST.json`). It is used only if
  `available_utc = valid_utc + 10 min <= captured_at_utc` and `is_cor == False`. The running max covers the target local date
  only.
- `h.assert_point_in_time` passed for the latest obs used at every covered snapshot. Every earlier obs used for a slope is
  older. In code, every covered snapshot has `t - valid >= 10 min`.
- Sky and wind are inputs only, taken from the same PIT row.
- No market field enters any candidate (rule 2), and no winner or settlement_high (rule 3).
- History labels are METAR day maxima for days <= 2026-07-31, so all fitted tables end on 2026-07-31.
- The only quantity fitted on this table is r3's w, and it was fitted on the before stratum.
- No NBM or NBP guidance is involved, and no hour gate is chosen (rule 8). r3's w is global.
- IEM 1-minute and 5-minute data are not used.
- Sensitivity at +30 min availability (t4-d1): the 13-16 and 17-23 LEADs survive for both r1 and r3. r3's 10-12 LEAD drops to
  WEAK: -0.004875, upper bound +0.000262. Treat 10-12 as fragile.

## Serveability

**Class: needs new capture.**

- **What to capture at each snapshot:** the station METAR/SPECI stream, meaning current tmpf, the running max since local
  midnight (including the midnight carry-over), and the obs from 1 h and 3 h earlier. r1 also needs sky and wind; r2 needs
  neither.
- **Source:** a free public METAR feed, such as aviationweather.gov or IEM.
- **Cadence:** the snapshot cadence (about 9 min), or one poll per new METAR (about 1-3 per hour per station).
- **Cost:** negligible, KB per day.
- **Artifact:** a frozen remaining-rise lookup table per station x month (fitted <= 2026-07-31). r3 also needs w = 0.8.

Production already captures `high_so_far` / `trusted_current_max`, but F3 shows that `high_so_far` misses the midnight
carry-over in 00-09. This candidate uses the METAR running max from local midnight. Whether production's existing observation
capture can stand in for it is an open question for T20. If it can, r2/r3 become a zero-parameter serving stage over a frozen
table. No retrain of the served model is needed.
