**Read this first.** t3-r3 is a ladder rung, not a new idea. It is the simplest statement of the T1/T2 mechanism that F3 found: served keeps mass above the running max late in the day. ## Rules (registered in C:\swarm\registry.jsonl before the first score; agent t3)

Common to all rules:
- **Hourly row.** An IEM asos.py routine METAR, COR excluded. temp_f = round-half-up(T-group C x 1.8 + 32), falling back to the body integer C.
- **Availability.** valid + 10 min (A-IEM-1 basis). A row enters only if it is available <= captured_at_utc.
- **Daily hourly-row max.** The max over routine rows of the station-local date. A complete day needs >= 20 routine rows.
- **History.** Local dates 2024-05-01..2026-07-31. Each station-month sample covers the Aug/Sep of 2024 and 2025, about 52-62 days.
- **Constants fixed a priori, never tuned:** kernel sd 1.0 F, history running max taken at local H:30, minimum 20 rows.
- **Scoring.** The integer pmf is mapped onto the bands. The harness applies the floor mask, and uncovered snapshots fall back to served.

The rules:
- **t3-r1 climatology.** The station x target-month pmf of the daily hourly-row max, smoothed with a discrete Gaussian (sd 1 F).
- **t3-r2 persistence.** N(c, sd), discretised. c = yesterday's PIT hourly-row max, which needs >= 20 available rows, otherwise served fallback. sd = the history sd of dmax(d) - dmax(d-1) for the station and month: 2.6 F at MIA up to 8.3 F at BKF in Sep.
- **t3-r3 floor + remaining rise.** R = today's PIT running max of routine rows. P(high = R + e) uses the history pmf of e = dmax - running max at local H:30, by station x month x local hour: 3,036 cells, minimum n 45. It is smoothed with sd 1 and negative e is folded onto 0. With no row yet today it falls back to served, which happens mostly at local hour 0.
- **t3-r3s.** t3-r3 with every METAR availability delayed a further 60 min, both at serve time and in the history table. This is a robustness variant.

The multiplicity contribution is 4 registered rules.

## Point-in-time statement

- **Inputs.** The only inputs are METAR routine rows, which enter only when valid + 10 min <= captured_at_utc. The merge-asof join asserts this with `h.assert_point_in_time` on the available_utc of the latest row used. COR rows are excluded.
- **No other inputs.** No market fields, no winner or settlement_high, and no captured guidance feature are used, apart from the harness floor (rule 4).
- **Fitting.** All fitted quantities (climatology pmfs, persistence sd, remaining-rise pmfs) use local dates <= 2026-07-31 only.
- **Day completeness.** The "complete day" filter (>= 20 rows) is applied only to *history* labels. At serve time r3 uses only rows available at t, and r2 uses yesterday's rows available at t.
- **Rule 8.** No hour gate is chosen. Each rule applies at every hour, and the block breakdown is reporting only.
- **Caveat.** IEM keeps one row per valid time, so a COR may have replaced an original. Excluding COR leaves a gap, which is conservative.

## Interpretation

- **Climatology and persistence lose to served at every hour from 10 on**, and are about neutral earlier. Served is far better than knowing nothing, and persistence is only about as good as served overnight (00-09 at -0.001 to -0.004). These are the bottom rungs.
- **Floor + remaining rise beats served by a wide margin in the afternoon and evening.** It uses METAR alone plus a lookup table built from 2 prior summers.
- **Ladder rungs for the synthesis.** In the from stratum, 17-23, the market is at 0.0008, the r3 rung is about +0.008 above the market, and served is about +0.030 above it.
- **00-16 (what matters to the maker).** r3 closes 18% of the gap. The interval spans 0 (-0.0043 [-0.0121, +0.0017]), so it is WEAK.
- **Overlap with T1/T2/T4.** r3 is the unconditional version of T2 and implies a soft T1 collapse. Any T1/T2/T4 lead should be reported as marginal over t3-r3.

## Serveability

**t3-r3: zero-parameter serving stage.**
- **No fit on this table.** It needs a frozen lookup of 11 stations x 12 months x 24 hours of remaining-rise pmfs, built from public IEM METAR history <= 07-31, plus a PIT running max of routine hourly rows since local midnight.
- **Afternoon input.** Production already captures high_so_far / trusted_current_max, and F3 shows these agree with the METAR running max by 17-23 (mean -0.01 F).
- **Morning input differs.** In 00-09 the definitions differ: high_so_far misses the midnight carry-over. A strict implementation would need the routine-METAR-since-local-midnight running max. That is a small capture change to production's existing METAR read (hourly cadence, negligible cost). The afternoon leads do not depend on it.
- **Train/serve parity.** It replaces the served distribution where it applies, so it is a new stage, not a recalibration of served. Refreshing the table monthly from history is a design choice.

**t3-r1 and t3-r2:** not proposed (HARM or WEAK). Both would also be zero-parameter stages.
