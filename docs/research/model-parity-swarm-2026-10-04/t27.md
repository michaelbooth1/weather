## 3. The S7 gate: necessary, helpful or neutral?

The critic's point is that LADDER r1b (faithful restore + taper + gate) minus r1 is only +0.0004 at 17-23, which
contradicts "the gate is required".

**Mechanism (production code):**
- Production runs the lock-in L_s first (`model_distribution.py:518`), then the calibration p^(1/T'), with
  T' = 1 + (T−1)(1−s) (`calibration_runtime.py:380-381`).
- The s it uses is **the same `lockin_strength`** (`model_distribution.py:544`).
- So at s = 1, T' = 1 and the calibration is the identity: it adds exactly 0 above the high.
- At s < 1 it adds a residual that shrinks with s.
- The payload's 0.031 -> 0.087 is the **s = 0 case**, which is the defect state itself: there is no lock-in, so
  there is no taper.

**ATL 2026-09-20 23:55, from this table's served bands.**
- I inverted the served bands to pre-calibration buckets with ATL's T = 1.3 (cutoff hour 20). That gives
  above-high mass **0.034 before calibration and 0.087 after**, against the payload's 0.031 -> 0.087. This
  validates the inversion.
- The table gives the above-high mass (bands above B = 88 F) after the restored lock-in at strength s, then what
  calibration adds:

**So r1b's +0.0004 is not the gate.** It is the ordering: faithful lock-in then taper, versus r1's lock-in applied
after calibration. The gate alone is slightly helpful. The two ingredients are worth about 0.3% and 0.6% of the
from-stratum served − market gap at 17-23 (+0.0298). Every figure here is an emulation from inverted bands. Only a
replay through `estimate_distribution` confirms it.

**Wording for the owner:**

> Restoring the lock-in is the fix. The calibration taper (S7) needs no separate change: it already reads the same
> lock-in strength (`model_distribution.py:544`), so restoring the strength restores the taper. With the lock-in
> restored at strength s, the untapered calibration would put back roughly 0.02 of probability above the high at
> s = 0.9 and 0.001 at s = 1. The production taper puts back 0.0013 at s = 0.9 and nothing at s = 1. Across the
> evening rows (mean s = 0.96) that is +0.010 untapered vs +0.001 tapered. The ATL 23:55 payload (0.031 -> 0.087)
> shows the s = 0 defect state. It is not evidence that a restored lock-in needs a further gate. An extra gate on S7
> is optional: it removes the last ~0.001 of above-high mass and improves 17-23 Brier by about 0.0001. That is
> helpful but negligible, and not required. The one thing that IS required is that the restored strength reaches
> the taper: if the lock-in were re-implemented as a separate post-calibration step, or the taper kept reading the
> WU-anchored strength (still 0), the calibration would re-add about 0.010 above the high at 17-23 (Delta Brier
> +0.00017). That is still small next to the lock-in's −0.025. Proposal only; the serving fix is an owner decision.
