## The rule (registered before the first score; agent t18)

**Model.** t18-r1 is a "rung x guidance likelihood" EMOS:

P(H = R+e) proportional to p0(e) * exp(-0.5*((R+e-m)/sigma)^2), for e = 0..40 F.

**Base, unchanged from t3-r3:**
- R is the PIT running max of routine non-COR IEM METAR rows of the local target date. A row counts once it is available (valid + 10 min) at or before captured_at.
- p0 is the history remaining-rise pmf by station x month x local hour, built from local dates <= 2026-07-31.
- As sigma -> infinity the model reduces exactly to t3-r3. As a check, the rebuilt rung reproduces the t3-r3 17-23 from figure, -0.021906, exactly.

**Mean.** m = mu_rr + a + sum_k b_k (max(S_k, R) - mu_rr), where mu_rr = R + E_p0[e] and sigma = exp(c).
- A source value that is missing or not point in time is imputed as mu_rr, so its term is 0.
- There is one coefficient set for all hours, so no hour gate (rule 8). The hour dependence comes only from the history rung: the rung prior is sharp in the evening, so it dominates the guidance there.

**Source selection.**
- The pool was captured v2_mean, hrrr_high, nws_grid_high, forecast_high, and the NBH latest-cycle remaining-hours max (t5-r1 machinery).
- Sources were chosen by greedy forward selection on before-stratum training NLL over the fitted no-source control c0. A source had to gain at least 0.001 nats per snapshot, with at most 3 chosen.
- This is the "marginal value over the rung" criterion, applied on the training set only.

**Fit.**
- (a, b_k, c) minimise the mean NLL of the integer METAR daily hourly-row max (a complete day needs at least 20 routine rows). No winner or settlement field is used.
- The fit uses before-stratum snapshots, target dates 2026-08-01..08-22: 38,660 rows. The optimiser is L-BFGS-B.

## Interpretation

1. **Is this the remaining-rise family in disguise?** In 17-23, yes: there is no increment over the rung. In 00-16, no: the rung alone is WEAK there (-0.0043), and the increment over the rung comes from multiplying in captured NBM v2_mean. That is the known 79a/81a/111h "served under-uses its own captured v2 guidance" family (EF 10h/10j/10p), now applied as a likelihood on the METAR rung rather than on its own.
   - An unpaired comparison, for scale only: R-T5-INC's zero-parameter v2 Gaussian c1 scores 00-16 -0.0100 vs served, and t18-c1 scores -0.0134. The difference, about -0.003, would be the rung's contribution to the morning and afternoon on top of v2. It is unpaired and post hoc, and I did not establish it as a paired increment.
2. **"Best three sources" collapses to two: METAR (the rung) and NBM v2.** HRRR, the served forecast_high, the NWS grid and NBH add nothing measurable once v2 is in. No new capture is justified by T18: NBH was screened and not selected.
3. **forecast_high has a negative coefficient (-0.27).** That is a correction to the served model's own output, and its increment is null. A served form should drop it. The defensible draft is the one-source form (rung x v2). c1 was registered after reading r1, so the draft must be a NEW prospective pre-registration on new dates (rule 7). Development numbers here are not evidence for it.
4. **Power.** The from 00-16 MDE80 is about 0.0076 per the harness, and the observed -0.0140 is larger than that. Even so, there are only 11 market clusters (EF 10j caveat), and the from dates were previously read.
