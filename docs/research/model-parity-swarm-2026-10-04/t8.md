  f00-f12 LastModified is 52-67 min after the cycle. h.assert_point_in_time passed on all 110,077 covered snapshots.

    the label uses, but on fit dates only.

## Serveability

- **Single-Runs remaining-hours HRRR: no capture case.**
  - The morning gain is available from the captured hrrr_high (c2), which would be a zero-parameter serving stage
    (sigma borrowed from r2; zero-parameter only with a fixed sigma). It falls in the same family as the morning route
    MG-1 (captured v2_mean). Any use needs a NEW pre-registration on new dates; this is a development read on
    previously inspected dates.
  - The afternoon and evening gains belong to the remaining-rise / defect-restoration family.
- Capturing an hourly HRRR series in production, for a remaining-hours max, would cost one Open-Meteo hourly call per
  station per snapshot and needs no key. It is not justified by this evidence.
- t8-r2, -r3 and -r4 would also need fitted constants (retrain class). r1 has zero parameters.
