  - "agree": markets where the tail sign matches the all-row per-market sign.
  - "worst": the lowest per-market tail share.

## Caveats

- **Development only.** The from stratum was previously inspected (rule 7). Tail rows are selected on the realised outcome, so tail figures are descriptive. They must not be used to rank or select.
- **No interval on per-market shares.** They are point ratios of sums over each market's tail rows.
- **Non-tail is a derived scale.** It is expressed in units of the tail excess (tail improvement divided by the point share removed).
