## Comparison method

I walked every numeric leaf of C:\swarm\out\t23\t23_t5r1_reimpl.score.json against C:\swarm\out\t5\t5_r1_nbh_latest.score.json with tolerance 1e-12. There were 2 differences, both in reason_counts:
- T5: absent 1,408.
- T23: absent 704 + missing_floor 704 = 1,408.

T5 dropped the 704 no-floor snapshots itself; I passed them and the harness removed them as missing_floor. The scored set is the same 109,399 snapshots (98.73%). My own coverage reasons were 110,103 ok and 704 uncovered_hour; no_eligible_cycle and no_tsd_at_argmax never fired.

## Interpretation choices the registry text leaves open (for the pre-registration drafter)

1. "Latest eligible cycle": I took the largest cycle_utc among objects with S3 LastModified <= captured_at_utc, not the latest LastModified. This matches T5 (bit-exact).
2. Ties at the argmax hour: I took the earliest valid hour v (this matches T5). The text does not say which tied hour supplies TSD, and the choice is NOT innocuous:
   - 55,548 of 110,807 snapshots have a tied maximum.
   - Choosing the latest tied hour instead would change sigma in 15,398 snapshots (13.9%).
   - Choosing the maximum TSD among the ties would change it in 11,374 snapshots. By block: 00-05 4,779; 06-09 3,037; 10-12 2,188; 13-16 1,119; 17-23 251.
   - I did not score these alternatives, because spares register no new rules. A prospective pre-registration must state the tie rule explicitly.
3. Local midnight E: I used the snapshot's own UTC offset from captured_at_local; there is no DST change in Aug-Sep.
4. v grid: top-of-hour UTC times in (t, E], with E included. All NBH valid times are on the hour (asserted).
5. TSD missing at the argmax hour: the rule falls back. This never occurred.
6. Band probabilities come from the CDF of H = max(B, X): F_H(k) = 0 for k < B, otherwise Phi((k + 0.5 - mu)/sigma). Then eq = F_H(high) - F_H(low - 1), lte = F_H(high), gte = 1 - F_H(low - 1).

## Point in time and rules

- Rule 1: h.assert_point_in_time passes on the max LastModified of every cycle used by each of the 110,103 covered snapshots. That includes older cycles carrying individual v, not only the newest one. Only valid times after t are used.
- Rules 2 and 3: no market or settlement input. Inputs are h.candidate_inputs() plus NBH TMP/TSD from C:\swarm\data\nbh\nbh_tidy.parquet (tidy sha256 0124b674...ad971).
