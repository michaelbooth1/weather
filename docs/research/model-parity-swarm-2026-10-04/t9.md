## Interpretation
1. **17-23 is the family in disguise.** The HRRR gain over the t3-r3 rung (-0.0061, 11/11) equals the no-source control's gain over t3-r3 (-0.0065). That gain belongs to the Gaussian max(F, Y) collapse form, not to HRRR. Per the 01:50 steering, this is the D-DEFECT serving defect and needs no refuters.
2. **The morning HRRR information is real but already captured.** HRRR carries morning information over a source-free remaining-rise centre: r1 - c1 is about -0.012/-0.014 in 00-09, with 11/11 markets and both strata. Production already has it: the captured hrrr_high control matches it (r1 - c2 ~0), and the captured NBM v2_mean is better still (r1 - c1_v2 +0.006..+0.008 in 00-12). Against the t3-r3 rung, the 00-16 gain is -0.0021 [-0.0068, +0.0032], which is not significant.
3. **13-16 is the remaining-hours form, not the source.** In 13-16, r1 is better than both day-max controls (c2 -0.0059 and v2 c1 -0.0082, about 7-9/11 markets). The source is not what helps: r1 is not better than t3-r3 (+0.0030), and r1 - c1 is -0.0034 (NS). The gain is the remaining-hours truncation (the T5/D-DEFECT 15-16 upper-truncation family), not HRRR.
4. **Time lagging and spread add nothing.** The lagged ensemble is indistinguishable from the latest single run, the spread-inflated sigma is indistinguishable from the fixed hour sigma, and the spread-skill correlation is 0.18. The residual sd is 2.2-2.9 F. HRRR's station bias ranges from -1.9 F (HOU) to +1.5 F (AUS), and a station x hour bias was NOT registered or scored.
