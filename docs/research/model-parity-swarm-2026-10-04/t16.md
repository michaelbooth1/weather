### Why the headroom route is void: a structural finding

The idea was to model how the venue rounds each hourly row. On 10 of 11 stations, ASOS T-group values are whole-degF sensor readings re-encoded as tenths degC: 99.91-100.00% of T-group rows convert back to within +/-0.09 F of an integer. So the half-up rounding to degF is an identity on the sensor's own whole-degF values. The tenths carry no sub-degree position, and every live snapshot outside Denver falls in the middle headroom bin (H about 42-58 hundredths).

The exception is **KBKF (Denver/Buckley), which reports true tenths**: only 18.1% of its rows sit near an integer. All 38 of F2's .5 ties are there. Even at Denver, r1 - r2 per-market is -0.00019 in 13-16 and 0.00000 in 17-23.

Conclusion: **the hourly-row mechanics are about sampling (which minutes get reported), not rounding.** The settled value is the max of whole-degF readings at routine and SPECI report times.
