## Rules (full text in the registry)

**r1: upper bound only.**
- Predictor: X = t850_adiab_sfc_f, the 850 hPa temperature taken dry-adiabatically down to the RAOB surface pressure.
- Sounding: 12Z on the target date, from the first site with a 12Z launch in the A-Soundings market_to_raob order. KEDW is excluded.
- Bound: U = X + q0.99(R), where R = METAR daily max - X.
- Fit: R is fitted per market on local dates 2026-05-01..07-31 only, with at least 30 fit days.
- Action: bands with low > U get zero mass; the rest is renormalised.

**Variants:**
- r2: r1 plus a lower bound L = X + q0.01(R); bands with high < L get zero mass.
- r3: r2 with q0.975 and q0.025.
- r4: r2, falling back to the same-day 18Z sounding (available 19Z, with separately fitted 18Z quantiles) when no 12Z exists.
- r5: r2 with X = t925_adiab_sfc_f, the predictor with the narrowest residual width on the fit window (chosen on history only).

The harness applies the floor. A row with no sounding, no fit, or zero remaining mass falls back to served (the primary all-row estimand).

- No evaluation labels, no market input, no winner, no settlement_high.

## Interpretation
- After the history-fitted 1-99% margin, the bound spans about +-10F. That is too wide to inform a 2F band distribution, and served (NBM-driven) mass rarely sits beyond it.
- Where the bound binds in the afternoon or evening, the PIT METAR running max and the floor already carry the same information.
- The lower bound actively removes winning mass.
- The 17-23 decided-band problem (F3) is not a sounding problem: a morning sounding cannot say the high is already in.
- Treat the sounding route as closed at this resolution.
