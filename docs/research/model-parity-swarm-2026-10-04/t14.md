# T14 Cloud conditioner on remaining rise: verdict WEAK (development)

**Verdict: WEAK.** METAR or GOES sky cover adds almost nothing to the remaining-rise forecast.

- **Best cloud rule: t14-r4, the low-cloud tilt of served.** Its sign is negative in every block and in both strata, but every effect is about -0.0005 to -0.0011. It misses the LEAD rule's interval and size conditions everywhere and reaches only 6-7 of the 11 markets.
- **Best block: 10-12.** From-stratum all-row r4 - served is -0.00111 [-0.00262, +0.00019], against a 5%-of-gap bar of -0.00114. 7 of 11 markets are negative.
- **t14-r1 and t14-r2 classify as LEAD in 13-16 and 17-23, but that LEAD is not a cloud effect.** The registered control t14-c1 uses the same remaining-rise climatology with no sky class, and it gives the same numbers and the same LEAD classes. The paired r1 - c1 difference measures what the sky class adds. It is about 0 in every block and stratum, with tight intervals:
  - 13-16 from: -0.00009 [-0.00100, +0.00062]
  - 17-23 from: +0.000007 [-0.00004, +0.00006]
  - all pooled: -0.00003 [-0.00035, +0.00031]

  That LEAD therefore belongs to the T2/T1 remaining-rise and decided-band family. It is not a T14 finding, and it must not be counted twice in the synthesis.

## Rules (registered in C:\swarm\registry.jsonl at 00:47:13 and 00:48:46 local, before the first score)

**Shared definitions for every rule**
- R = final METAR hourly-row max minus the point-in-time running max M at snapshot t.
- The final max uses rounded tmpf from routine and SPECI reports, excluding COR, on the station's local date.
- The R climatology is fitted on IEM METAR local dates up to 2026-07-31. It uses months 7-10 of 2024, 2025 and July 2026, about 272-277 days per station.
- Cells are station x target-month window x local hour x deficit bin (M - current: <=0, 1-2, 3-5, 6+) x sky class.
- A cell needs at least 40 days. Otherwise it backs off to (hour, deficit), then to (hour).
- Sky classes:
  - 0: CLR, SKC, NSC, FEW or no layer.
  - 1: SCT.
  - 2: BKN, OVC or VV.

**Rules**

| id | rule |
|---|---|
| t14-r1 | Standalone nowcast: band masses of P(R \| ..., sky_all), shifted by M. sky_all = maximum cover over all layers. |
| t14-r2 | p_served x r1 band mass, renormalised. |
| t14-r3 | **Cloud tilt:** p_served x [band mass under P(R \| hour, deficit, sky_all) / band mass under P(R \| hour, deficit)], renormalised. Only rows with a populated sky cell are changed; the rest fall back to served. |
| t14-r4 | Same as r3, but the sky class uses only layers with base <= 10,000 ft (low and mid cloud). |
| t14-r5 | Same as r3, but the sky class comes from GOES ABI-L2-ACMC cloud_frac_3x3: below 0.25 -> 0, 0.25-0.75 -> 1, above 0.75 -> 2. The thresholds were fixed a priori. It uses the METAR all-layer tables, so nothing is fitted on GOES. |
| t14-c1 | Attribution control, not a candidate: r1 without the sky class. |

## Point-in-time statement

**METAR inputs**
- A METAR or SPECI row enters snapshot t only if available_utc = valid_utc + 10 min <= captured_at_utc, is_cor is False, and its local date equals the target date.
- This is checked vectorised for every snapshot, and with `h.assert_point_in_time` on a 3,000-snapshot sample.

**GOES inputs**
- A GOES scene enters only if available_utc <= t and the scan started no more than 75 min before t. available_utc = max(scene end, S3 LastModified); the measured median is 3.4 min after scan start.
- `h.assert_point_in_time` is called on every 50th covered snapshot.

**Training and serving**
- The history grid and the live snapshots use the same state function (train/serve parity).
- Fitted tables use dates up to 2026-07-31 only.

**Excluded inputs**
- No market input and no winner or settlement value, by construction: only allow-listed `h.candidate_inputs` columns are read.
- The history label (the final METAR max) comes only from history dates.
- No IEM 1-minute or 5-minute data is used.
- The floor is applied by the harness. Uncovered rows fall back to served, and the all-row estimand is primary.

## Serveability

- **r4 and r5 (cloud tilts):** a *zero-parameter serving stage* applied on top of served, with lookup tables frozen from history up to 07-31.
  - r4 needs no new capture beyond PIT METAR sky-cover groups. Production already ingests METAR, but it would need the skyc/skyl layers carried into features.
  - r5 *needs new capture*: GOES ABI-L2-ACMC CONUS, hourly or faster, anonymous S3. Each scene is processed in memory and only the 3x3 pixels are kept, with about 3.4 min latency.
  - The value is too small to justify either.
- **r1, r2 and c1:** remaining-rise climatology, a zero-parameter serving stage on frozen tables. They are the T2 family; serveability belongs to T2.

## Caveats

- The from stratum was read previously (rule 7). It is a development read, not a holdout.
- The sky class at each snapshot is the latest METAR's. ASOS ceilometers see only up to 12,000 ft, so "CLR" can hide high cloud. That is the reason r4 uses low layers only.
- GOES is uncorrected for parallax, and night-time ACM is IR-only.
- COR exclusion can leave a gap in the hourly rows; the previous report is then used.
- The r1 and r2 classifier LEAD is reported exactly as the harness classified it, and attributed through the registered control c1.
